Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal information MASAÉ collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

MASAÉ is a brand of Awwadh International, based at 110, Vikas Nagar, Lakhan pur, Kanpur – Uttar Pradesh – 208024, India. In this policy, “MASAÉ”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Awwadh International in its role as the owner of the MASAÉ brand and of the website masae.in (the “Site”).

2. What we collect

We keep the information we collect to the minimum needed to answer you and to fulfil your orders.

  • Details you give us when you write to us. Your name, email address, phone number and the message you write on our Contact page.
  • Details you share to place an order. Orders are placed through WhatsApp. To process and deliver an order we need your name, phone number and delivery address, and the products you want.
  • Newsletter sign-up. If you subscribe to The MASAÉ Letter, your email address.
  • Technical information. Like every website, when your browser loads the Site, the servers that deliver it receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request.
  • Preferences stored on your own device. The Site remembers whether you have muted the background music, and where the music had reached, using your browser’s local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect payment card or bank details through the Site. The Site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

3. How the contact form works

The form on our Contact page does not send your details to a server of ours. When you press Send message, the Site opens WhatsApp with your message already filled in, addressed to our business number. Nothing reaches us until you press send inside WhatsApp. Once you do, the message is handled by WhatsApp (operated by Meta) under its own terms and privacy policy, as well as this one.

The newsletter form works in a similar way: it opens a pre-filled email to info@masae.in, and your address reaches us only if you send that email.

4. How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and questions.
  • To confirm, prepare, deliver and keep records of your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To send The MASAÉ Letter, only if you asked to receive it. You can opt out at any time.
  • To keep the Site secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed and only as far as it is needed:

  • Delivery partners (couriers and postal services) receive your name, phone number and address so they can deliver your order.
  • Payment providers or banks that handle the payment you choose to make for an order.
  • Service providers that help us run the Site and our communications, bound to use the information only for that purpose.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to disclose information.

6. Third-party services on the Site

To work, the Site loads some resources from other providers. When it does, your browser connects to them directly and they will see your IP address and basic browser details:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), for our typefaces.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs and jsDelivr, which deliver the open-source animation libraries the Site uses.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Site.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram (Meta), when you follow a link from our Site to them.

These providers have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

7. How long we keep it

We keep messages and order records only for as long as needed to deal with your request, to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements, and to resolve any dispute. After that we delete them or anonymise them.

8. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information of yours we hold and how we use it;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • erase your information, where we no longer need it for a lawful purpose;
  • stop sending you The MASAÉ Letter or other updates, by telling us or replying “unsubscribe”.

To use any of these rights, write to info@masae.in. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

The Site and our products are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, please tell us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
