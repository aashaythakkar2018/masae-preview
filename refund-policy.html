Refund Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

We want you to choose with confidence, so we are clear about this before you order: every MASAÉ sale is final.

No refunds. MASAÉ does not give refunds of any kind, in any circumstance, against any order. We also do not accept returns, exchanges or cancellations once an order has been confirmed.

1. What this means

Once we have confirmed your order on WhatsApp:

  • no part of the price you paid, including delivery charges, will be refunded;
  • the order cannot be cancelled, changed or exchanged for another fragrance or product;
  • products cannot be returned to us, and we will not collect returned parcels.

This applies whatever the reason, including a change of mind, ordering the wrong fragrance or quantity, not liking a fragrance, or a delay in delivery. It also applies to the Discovery Set and to every other MASAÉ product.

2. Why we work this way

MASAÉ incense is a fragrance product, and once a packet has been dispatched we cannot resell it. Scent is also personal, which is why we would rather help you choose well than promise a refund afterwards.

3. Before you order

Please take a moment to be sure:

  • Read the fragrance notes on the Shop page, and ask us if you are unsure. Tell us about the room and the mood, and we will suggest a fragrance.
  • If you would like to try several scents, the Discovery Set is a good way to begin.
  • Check the products, quantity, price and delivery address in your WhatsApp confirmation. They are final once confirmed.

4. If something is wrong with your parcel

If your parcel arrives damaged or you believe there is a problem with it, write to us at info@masae.in or on WhatsApp at +91 91706 63334, with your order details and, if you can, a photograph. We will look into it and reply. Raising a concern does not create a right to a refund, and our Terms of Service still apply.

5. Your legal rights

This policy is part of our Terms of Service. It does not limit any right you have under law that cannot be excluded, including your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

6. Contact us

Questions about this policy? We are happy to explain it before you order. Write to info@masae.in or message us on WhatsApp.
